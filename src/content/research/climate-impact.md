I want to know how a warmer climate will change the water we depend on. I run CMIP6 projections through land-surface models and use statistical tools such as vine copulas to look at future runoff and drought risk.

I have worked on the Upper Bhima, Sina, Mahanadi, Narmada and Godavari basins. I have also looked at flood hotspots, urban heatwaves, and how reservoirs and groundwater can soften some of these changes.
