How will a warmer climate change the water we depend on? Using CMIP6 projections, land-surface models and multivariate statistics such as vine copulas, I study projected changes in water-balance components, hydrological drought risk, flood hotspots and urban heatwaves — and how storage structures and groundwater systems can buffer those changes.
