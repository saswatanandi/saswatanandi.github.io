Most of my work runs through land-surface models. I have calibrated the VIC model with a self-adaptive differential evolution method, which helps when each model run is slow. I have coupled VIC with the RAPID river routing model and the LISFLOOD-FP flood model to simulate streamflow and map flooded areas.

I have also tested how well satellite rainfall and evapotranspiration products work as model inputs. Much of this work is in Indian basins such as the Upper Bhima and Krishna.
