Land-surface models are how we turn weather into water. My work spans the full chain: calibrating computationally intensive, distributed models with self-adaptive differential evolution; coupling the VIC land-surface model with the RAPID river-routing scheme and the LISFLOOD-FP hydrodynamic model for streamflow and flood-inundation mapping; and evaluating how satellite rainfall and evapotranspiration estimates perform as model inputs.
