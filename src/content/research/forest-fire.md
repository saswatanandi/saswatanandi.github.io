Forests in the Sierra Nevada use a lot of water. Thinning, prescribed burns and wildfires change how much they use, and so how much ends up in the rivers. I use high-resolution hydrological models and satellite data to measure these changes.

One study looked at how forest management changes the long-term water balance of the upper Kings River Basin. Other work looks at how wildfires change evapotranspiration across California.
