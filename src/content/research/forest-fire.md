Forests are the water towers of California. Thinning, prescribed burns and increasingly severe wildfires change how much water forests use and how much reaches rivers downstream. I combine high-resolution hydrological modelling with remote sensing to quantify these changes across the water–energy gradient of the Sierra Nevada — from the effect of forest management on the long-term water balance of the upper Kings River Basin to how wildfires alter evapotranspiration patterns statewide.
