# Redirects from the old site

The old site (Hugo, zzo theme) served every page under `/en/`. Each address below has a small
redirect page on the new site (meta refresh, canonical link and a visible link), generated by Astro
from `src/lib/redirects.mjs`. Edit that file to add or change one. Redirect pages are left out of
the sitemap and of site search.

Any other `/en/...` address reaches the 404 page, which tries the same address without `/en/`
(`/en/posts/x` becomes `/blog/x`, `/en/softwares/x` becomes `/software/x`,
`/en/publication/x` becomes `/publications/x`, `/en/categories/x` becomes `/tags/x`, and
underscores in post names become hyphens) before showing "Page not found".

Old publication folders were matched to BibTeX keys by venue and year:
`joh_2022` Journal of Hydrology 2022, `h20_2020` H2Open Journal 2020, `espr_2022` Environmental
Science and Pollution Research (heatwaves, online 2022), `gc_2022` Geocarto International 2022,
`ewra_2017` European Water 2017, `wrm_2020` Water Resources Management 2020, `cciwr_2021` Climate
Change Impacts on Water Resources, `wmwg_2021` Water Management and Water Governance, `picsnc_2021`
Proceedings of the International Conference on Scientific and Natural Computing, `rfict_2018` Recent
Findings in Intelligent Computing Techniques, `igarss_2017` IGARSS 2017, `i2ct_2017` I2CT 2017.

Not redirected: the old RSS feeds (`/en/index.xml`, `/en/posts/index.xml`). A redirect page cannot
stand in for a feed; the new feed is `/rss.xml`.
